# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a respectful and harassment-free environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of different viewpoints.
- Give and receive constructive feedback professionally.
- Focus discussion on the project and technical issues.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spam or off-topic promotion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who violate this code of conduct.
